ActivBite Breakfast Bar 3D Mockup

Files:
- ActivBite_breakfast_bar_3D_mockup.glb: textured GLB model. Recommended for Blender, Sketchfab, Windows 3D Viewer, Babylon, Three.js, etc.
- ActivBite_breakfast_bar_shape_only.stl: shape-only STL for CAD/3D printing fallback. Textures are not included in STL.
- ActivBite_breakfast_bar_shape_only.obj: shape-only OBJ fallback.
- activbite_front_texture.png: front label texture created from the supplied artwork.
- activbite_back_texture.png: underside/back texture crop created from the supplied wrapper artwork.
- ActivBite_3D_mockup_preview.png: quick preview PNG.

Mockup details:
- Pillow-pack snack bar body
- Serrated ends
- Crimp ribs and sealed seams
- Curved wrapper surface with supplied ActivBite artwork

Note: The GLB is the main deliverable because it preserves the textured packaging look.
